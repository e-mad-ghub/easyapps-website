easyQR - your QRWallet

Create, save, scan, share, and split expenses with local-first QR tools.

QR tools for everyday use

easyQR keeps practical QR codes close, readable, and ready when you need them. It is built for quick personal workflows, from saved Wi-Fi codes and contact details to split settlements and Smart Message QRs.

QR Wallet

Save useful QR codes locally and show them quickly without needing an account.

Smart Message QR

Share readable messages through a QR code. The receiver opens a clean easyQR message page with Copy and Share actions.

QR Creation

Create plain text, website, email, phone, SMS, location, contact, Wi-Fi, event, WhatsApp message, and SEPA payment QRs.

Import Existing QR

Import QR codes from camera or gallery while preserving the original QR image.

Split Expenses

Split shared expenses, calculate who pays whom, and generate settlement messages and payment QRs.

Backup and App Lock

Backup your QR Wallet and protect the app with device authentication.

Pro Upgrade

Choose an optional one-time Pro upgrade for unlimited QR Wallet saving, QR customization, backup/restore, and Pro styling.

Local-first by design

easyQR works locally first. No account, no ads, no analytics, and no easyQR backend storing your QR Wallet data.

Smart Message reader

Smart Message QRs generated by easyQR open the message reader when scanned. The message stays in the URL fragment after #, is decoded locally in the browser, and is not uploaded or stored by the website.
