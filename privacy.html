EasyPass Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
EasyPass's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to Google API Service User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Data Access Disclosures

EasyPass explicitly requests access to your Google Drive account. This access is limited to the following data:

  • Folder Content: The app accesses a specific folder chosen or created by the user to store the membership database.
  • File Metadata & Content: The app reads and writes to "database.xlsx" and "config.json".

2. Data Use Disclosures

Your Google user data is used solely to provide the core functionality of the EasyPass app. We use this data to:

  • Synchronize member directories across multiple authorized devices.
  • Store organizational branding and security configurations.
  • Enable offline-first data management without the need for central servers.

3. Data Sharing Disclosures

We do not share, transfer, or disclose your Google user data to any third parties. Your data is stored exclusively in your own Google account and on your local device.

4. Data Protection & Security

EasyPass protects your sensitive data by leveraging Google's enterprise-grade security infrastructure and app-level AES-256 encryption.
