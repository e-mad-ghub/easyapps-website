EasyPass Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

EasyPass ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle your data through our Google Drive integration.

Google API Limited Use Disclosure:
EasyPass's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to Google API Service User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information Access and Usage

EasyPass requests access to your Google Drive storage to provide serverless synchronization for your membership directory. This access is used strictly to:

  • Create and update the "database.xlsx" file containing your member records.
  • Read and write the "config.json" file for organization settings and branding.

2. Data Storage and Privacy

All data managed by EasyPass is stored exclusively in your personal Google Drive account and on your local device. We do not operate any external servers, and we never collect, see, or transmit your data to any third parties.

3. Security

Your data is secured using industry-standard AES-256 encryption within the app and protected by Google's own security infrastructure on the cloud.
