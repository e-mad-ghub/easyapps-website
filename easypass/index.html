easyPass

A membership management application that helps organizations maintain member directories, issue digital QR membership passes, and verify membership status at entry points.

About Google Drive Access Privacy Policy

What easyPass Does

easyPass is designed for organizations that need a practical way to create, update, synchronize, and verify membership records. Authorized users can manage a member directory from supported devices, generate QR-based membership passes, and scan those passes to confirm membership status.

Main Features

Member Directory

Create and maintain membership records, including the information required by the organization to identify and manage its members.

QR Membership Passes

Generate digital QR passes associated with membership records for convenient presentation and verification.

Membership Verification

Allow authorized staff to scan a member's QR pass and verify the member's current status.

Google Drive Synchronization

Store and synchronize easyPass membership files using the Google Drive account authorized by the user.

Why easyPass Requests Google Drive Access

easyPass uses Google Drive only to provide its membership-data storage and synchronization functionality. After a user grants permission, easyPass may read, create, update, and synchronize the files used by the application, such as the membership database and application configuration files.

Google Drive data is used only for user-requested easyPass features. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or sale. Access can be revoked at any time from the user's Google Account permissions.

easyPass only requests Google permissions that are required for its declared functionality.

For complete details about the information easyPass accesses and how it is handled, read the easyPass Privacy Policy.
