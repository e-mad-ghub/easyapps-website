App Purpose & Functionality

easyPass is a professional membership management application that allows organizations to maintain a secure directory of their members with zero central server costs. The app provides a serverless infrastructure by leveraging the user's own cloud storage.

Core Features

  • Automated Directory Sync: Securely synchronizes your member list directly with a Google Drive folder in Excel format.
  • Digital Pass Generation: Creates professional QR-based digital access passes for all registered members.
  • Real-time Scanning: Allows authorized staff to scan and verify member digital passes instantly at entry points.

Google Drive Integration

easyPass requests access to the .../auth/drive scope to enable bi-directional synchronization between your mobile device and your secure Google Drive data hub. This ensures you maintain 100% ownership and privacy of your organization's data.
