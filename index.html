The EasyPass Solution

EasyPass is a secure, serverless membership management platform that allows organizations to maintain full ownership of their data. By integrating directly with a user's own Google Drive cloud storage, EasyPass provides:

  • Bi-directional synchronization of member directories via Excel.
  • Generation of professional digital QR access passes.
  • Real-time authorization scanning for staff members.
  • Complete privacy with zero central data storage.
