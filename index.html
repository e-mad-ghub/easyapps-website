The EasyPass Solution

EasyPass is a secure, serverless membership management platform. We eliminate complex server costs by using your own Google Drive cloud as a private data hub.

Key Functionalities:

  • Real-time synchronization of member directories via Excel.
  • Professional QR pass generation for organizations.
  • Authorized entrance scanning and member validation.
  • Full data ownership with zero central servers.
