EasyPass

Membership Management Simplified

Application Purpose

EasyPass is a mobile application designed to help organizations manage their membership directories and access control without the need for central servers.

How EasyPass Works

The app provides a serverless infrastructure by using the organization's own Google Drive as a private data hub. It allows authorized staff to:

  • Sync member lists securely via Excel files on Google Drive.
  • Issue digital QR passes to registered members.
  • Perform real-time scanning and validation at entry points.

By using the .../auth/drive scope, EasyPass ensures that users maintain 100% ownership and privacy of their data within their own Google account.
