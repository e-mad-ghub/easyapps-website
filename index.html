EasyPass

Android Membership Management Application

Purpose of the EasyPass App

EasyPass helps organizations manage and verify their members. The app lets an authorized organization administrator create and update a membership directory, store and synchronize that directory in the administrator's Google Drive, generate QR membership passes, and scan those passes to confirm whether a person has an active membership.

How It Works Google Drive Use Privacy Policy

How EasyPass Works

  1. Create member records

    An authorized administrator adds and maintains the organization's membership information.

  2. Store and synchronize

    EasyPass saves and synchronizes its membership files using a Google Drive account authorized by the user.

  3. Issue QR passes

    The organization generates a digital QR pass associated with each member record.

  4. Verify membership

    Authorized staff scan the QR pass to retrieve and confirm the member's current status.

Intended Users

EasyPass is intended for organizations, clubs, associations, facilities, and their authorized administrators or entry staff. It is not a general-purpose Google Drive file manager. Google Drive access is used specifically for EasyPass membership storage and synchronization.

Why EasyPass Requests Google Drive Data

EasyPass requests Google Drive permission so the user can use their own Google account as the storage location for the EasyPass membership database and configuration files.

After the user grants permission, EasyPass uses Google Drive access to:

  • Create or locate EasyPass application files.
  • Read membership records so they can be displayed and verified.
  • Write changes made by an authorized administrator.
  • Synchronize the same membership directory across authorized devices.

Google Drive data is used only to provide these EasyPass features. EasyPass does not use Google Drive data for advertising, sell it, or use it to build marketing profiles. Users can revoke access from their Google Account.

See the EasyPass Privacy Policy for full details about data access, use, storage, sharing, retention, deletion, and user control.
